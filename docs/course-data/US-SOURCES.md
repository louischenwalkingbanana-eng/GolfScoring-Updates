# GolfScoring US scorecard source extract

The distributed machine-readable database is `us-courses.json`, generated from `utils/course-data/open.js` by `export-us.cjs`. Course, source, tee name, unit, par and yards appear together. Empty city means the source did not supply one. Each card includes an individual data-record URL; available links to original published scorecards are included separately. Data are published scorecard values from dated sources, not live tournament setup or measurements made by GolfScoring.

- Selection: exactly 18 numbered scorecard positions per tee (some nine-hole facilities play two laps), complete par 3–5 and integer yards; no tee scaling or fill values. Where published whole-tee totals exist, they must match the 18 numbers exactly. OpenGolfAPI requires that total; a Yardage Hub tee published without a total can still be used if all 18 original numbers exist (shown total is arithmetic). Retained tees match source labels, with “(M)” or “(F)” only to distinguish published alternative pars for that tee.
- Sample taking: snapshot first; targeted US courses already in the app then deterministic round-robin draw across states. Same-layout duplicates resolved to one source/edition, first preferring Yardage Hub when an official card URL is present. Course and tee do not combine different source editions. Reproducers: `yardage-normalize.py`, `collect.py` and `merge.py`.
