GolfScoring Privacy

iPhone and iPad app · Updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains how the GolfScoring iPhone and iPad app handles information. It does not describe the WeChat edition or third-party websites.

Your scorecards stay on your device

GolfScoring saves the round names, dates, courses, shots, scores and Golfer Life progress you enter in the app’s local storage. Your language choice is also saved on the device. The app works offline and does not require an account.

No advertising, tracking or developer collection

The iPhone and iPad app does not send your scorecards or game progress to the developer. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell your information. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

Backups are your choice

When you choose Export backup, the app creates a JSON file and opens the system Files picker so you can choose where to save it. The file includes your scorecards and Golfer Life progress. When you choose Import backup, the app reads the file you select and asks before replacing its saved data. The app does not automatically synchronize records with other devices or WeChat.

A backup saved to iCloud Drive or another file provider is handled by that service under its own terms. Your device’s operating-system backup settings may also include app data. Exported JSON backups are not password protected by GolfScoring; store and share them only where you intend.

Keeping and deleting information

GolfScoring retains saved data locally until you change or remove it. You can delete individual scorecards in the app, keeping at least one card. The app may retain a previous local save for recovery. Deleting the app from your device removes its local app data, subject to your device’s backup and restore settings. Copies you exported or shared must be deleted separately from their saved locations.

Contacting us

If you email louischen40@outlook.com, your email address and whatever you choose to include are received for the purpose of answering your message. Please avoid attaching personal scorecard backups unless they are needed for your support request. Email is handled by your email provider and Outlook under their own policies.

Changes

This page will be updated if the app’s information handling changes. The date above identifies this version of the policy.

GolfScoring 隐私说明

iPhone 与 iPad 版本 · 更新于 2026 年 11 月 18 日

本说明介绍 GolfScoring iPhone 和 iPad 应用如何处理信息，不适用于微信小程序版本或第三方网站。

记分卡保存在设备上

您在应用中填写的球局名称、日期、球场、击球记录、成绩和“球手生活”进度保存在应用的本机存储中。语言选择也保存在设备上。应用可以离线使用，无需注册账号。

无广告、无跟踪、不向开发者上传数据

iPhone 和 iPad 应用不会将您的记分卡或游戏进度发送给开发者，不含广告或分析 SDK，不会跨应用或网站跟踪您，也不出售您的信息。应用不会请求读取通讯录、相机、麦克风或精确位置。

由您决定是否备份

选择“导出备份”后，应用生成包含记分卡及“球手生活”进度的 JSON 文件，并通过系统文件选择器让您决定保存位置。选择“导入备份”后，应用读取您选中的文件，并在替换原有记录之前请求确认。应用不会自动与其他设备或微信同步。

保存到 iCloud 云盘或其他文件服务的备份由相应服务按其规则处理。设备的系统备份设置也可能包含应用数据。GolfScoring 不为导出的 JSON 备份添加密码保护，请妥善保存和分享。

保存与删除

本机记录会保留，直到您修改或删除它们。您可以在应用中删除单张记分卡，并至少保留一张。应用可能保留上一份本机存档用于恢复。删除设备上的应用会删除其本机数据，具体恢复情况取决于设备的备份与恢复设置。已导出或分享的文件需要在相应保存位置另行删除。

联系我们

如果您向 louischen40@outlook.com 发送邮件，您的邮箱地址和主动提供的内容将用于回复。除非解决问题需要，请勿附带个人记分卡备份。邮件由您的邮件服务和 Outlook 按其各自规则处理。
