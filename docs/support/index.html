GolfScoring Support

Help with GolfScoring scorecards, shot records, Insights and JSON backups on iPhone and iPad.

louischen40@outlook.com

Contact us

For questions or a problem, email the app version, your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help. Please hide personal information and do not send a scorecard backup unless it is needed to diagnose your request.

Protect your scorecards

Scores are stored on your device. Use Export backup to save a JSON copy with the system Files picker before reinstalling or moving to another device. Choose Import backup in GolfScoring to restore a file. Importing replaces the app’s saved records after you confirm, so export your current data first if you want to keep it.

Using Insights

Record the ball’s lie and distance to the hole before each shot. Mark the final shot as holed out so its finishing position is known. Shots missing the information needed for measurement still count on your scorecard. Published benchmark ranges are explained in Insights. Performance comparisons are not an official Handicap Index.

Language

Use the English / 中文 buttons at the top of the app to change its display language. Your saved names and scorecards are preserved.

联系我们

如有问题，请通过邮件说明应用版本、设备型号、iOS 或 iPadOS 版本及问题出现的操作步骤。可以附上截图，但请先遮盖个人信息。除非排查问题需要，请勿发送个人记分卡备份。

保护您的记分卡

成绩保存在设备上。在重新安装应用或更换设备之前，请使用“导出备份”，通过系统文件选择器保存一份 JSON 文件。在 GolfScoring 中选择“导入备份”即可读取备份。导入会在您确认后替换应用当前记录；如需保留当前记录，请先导出。

使用技术分析

请记录每次击球前的球位和距洞距离，并将最后一杆标记为进洞，以确定击球终点。缺少分析所需信息的击球仍然计入记分卡杆数。可测范围和公开基准资料可在技术分析页面查看。表现对比不属于官方差点指数。

切换语言

使用应用顶部的 English / 中文按钮切换显示语言。已保存的名字和记分卡保持不变。
